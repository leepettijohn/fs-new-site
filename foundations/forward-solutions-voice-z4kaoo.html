Your Foundation was struck on , so this page isn’t about what the business is. It’s about how it sounds. I read every page of on and wrote down how it talks. Nothing here goes into your Brand Voice until you’ve confirmed it.

    Part one

    How the site sounds

    Each card is what I noticed, with the page it came from. Open a card. If it’s how you want to sound, tick it. If it isn’t, or it’s only half the story, press Copy to box and change the words until they’re true.

    Part two

    Where the site disagrees with itself

    Here the site says things that can’t all be true. Pick the one that’s true today. If none is quite right, copy the closest one into the box and fix it. Only you can settle this, so I haven’t guessed.

    Part three

    What the site couldn’t tell me

    No website answers these. Most boxes come with a few starting points, written the way a business like yours might answer. They are examples, not facts about you. Copy the closest one into the box, then change the details until it’s true. Or ignore them and write your own.

    Part four

    Words you won’t use

    These come from what your Foundation rules out. Tick Remove on any word you’re happy to use. Every word you don’t tick goes on the list. Add any that are missing in the box on each card.
