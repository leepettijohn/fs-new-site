    Part one

    What the site says

    Each card is a guess, with the page it came from. Open a card. If the guess is right, tick it. If it’s wrong, out of date or only half the story, press Copy to box and change the words until they’re true.

    The rules you seem to work by

    A rule only counts if it has cost you something: a job you turned down, hours you didn’t bill, a client you sent elsewhere. For each one, say whether it’s really a rule, and what it has cost you.

    What you won’t do

    For each one: is it a line you really hold?

    Part two

    Where the site disagrees with itself

    In each of these, the site says two or more things that can’t all be true. Pick the one that’s true today. If none is quite right, copy the closest one into the box and fix it. Only you can settle these, so I haven’t guessed.

    Part three

    What the site couldn’t tell me

    No website answers these. Each box comes with a few starting points, written the way a business like yours might answer. They are examples, not facts about you. Copy the closest one into the box, then change the details until it’s true. Or ignore them and write your own.
