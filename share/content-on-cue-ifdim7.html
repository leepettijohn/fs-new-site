Content on Cue

Content on Cue texts people at an organization on a schedule, asking for a photo, a video, or a thought about their work. They reply to the text. The reply is captured, tied to the right person and organization, stored for good, and waiting in a portal the agency works from. Their effort ends at hitting send.

Two

Where the photos and videos live

People get their files through the portal, and only through the portal. Nobody needs a Google account, and nobody is given access to where the files are stored.

  • Each texted photo is saved with its record in Airtable. That’s what lets the portal show a small preview and a Download button to whoever is logged in.
  • A copy of every file also goes to your Google Drive. That copy is yours, as your own archive. It stays private to you.
  • Who sees what is decided by the portal login. A company sees its own files and nobody else’s.
  • Files are copied the moment a reply arrives. ClickSend only holds a texted photo for 7 days, so nothing is left sitting there.
  • When a client leaves, they download their files from the portal before their last month of service ends, as you described.
  • If storage runs past what your Drive plan covers, that’s yours to bill on to the client.

How this screen behaves

  • A slot is a standing setting, not a one-off message. A Tuesday slot fires every Tuesday until you turn it off.
  • Randomize picks from the company’s prompts and skips anything sent recently, so a slot never needs restocking.

Four

How it runs

Four things happen behind the screens.

Setting up an organization

  1. Intake meetingYou meet with the organization and decide what to ask and who to ask.
  2. Add the company and its peopleName, phone numbers, roles.
  3. Give it prompts and slotsPulled from the library or written for them, then scheduled.
  4. Its upload link and Drive folder are createdThe upload link carries the company’s own code.
  5. The first prompt goes outOn the first scheduled slot.

Sending a prompt

  1. The scheduler checks what’s dueAirtable looks at every active slot. More than six hours late? Marked missed, and not sent.
  2. The slot is stamped as firedBefore anything is sent, so it can’t fire twice.
  3. The prompt is chosenThe specific one, a custom one, or a random pick from the company’s set.
  4. One send is created per personEach recipient gets their own row on the Sends screen.
  5. ClickSend delivers the text

Capturing a reply

  1. Someone replies by textWords or a photo.
  2. ClickSend passes it alongThe reply goes to Make the moment it arrives.
  3. The person is found by phone number Unknown number? It’s flagged for the agency.
  4. The reply is matched to its promptThe open send for that person is filled in. No open send? A new row is created and labeled unprompted.
  5. The photo is saved with the recordSo it shows up in the portal with a preview and a Download link.
  6. A copy goes to your DriveMake puts it in the right agency, company and month folder, and names it.
  7. The agency is told new content arrivedBy email, to whoever holds the agency role for that company.
