Onboarding is the Quick Win

Decided
  • $1,500, required before Core or Pro, and charged in addition to the monthly fee.
  • Order: send the Quick Win invoice, do the Quick Win, kick off Core or Pro, send that invoice, and start implementation once it is paid.
  • The Quick Win’s scope adds account access and connections, and tracking setup.
6

Overruns and out-of-scope requests

Decided
  • Inside the scope, task values are fixed. Whoever does the task absorbs any extra time.
  • Anything out of scope gets a change order. The person who will do the task decides whether to charge; the change order is written either way, with an amount or with $0.

8

The partner’s contract

Decided
  • Minimum term: 3 months of Core or Pro after the Quick Win, then month to month.
  • Cancellation: 30 days’ written notice.
  • Payment: monthly in advance; work pauses if a payment is 10 days late.
  • When a partner leaves: anything built on their own site and accounts is theirs once paid for. Articles on healthyhome.network stay on HHN. HHN’s templates and system stay HHN’s.
