3

When the work costs more than the line pays

HeldDecided

A task pays its value. If two people share it, they split it by who did what. What happens when the task takes more time than its value covers?

From the draft
  • Core sets aside $1,800 a month for labor: $1,300 direct implementation plus $500 strategy and account management (page 6). Pro sets aside $2,850 (page 8).
  • The guardrail: “If the account routinely requires materially more human labor, the scope or pricing must change.”
  • It does not say what “routinely” means or who makes the call.
Options
  1. Task values are fixed. A slow task pays the same, and the person doing it absorbs the extra time.
  2. Profit covers it. The person is paid for the real time and the margin shrinks.
  3. The scope or the price changes as soon as it happens.

Lee’s proposalOption a, with a trigger. If the work log shows a partner running over the time its task values were set for three months in a row, either of us can raise it at the monthly review, and we both agree on a change to the scope or the price.

4

Onboarding: is there a fee?

HeldDecided

Setting a partner up (access, tracking, the first funnel, the CRM connection) is front-loaded work. Is it charged separately or covered by the monthly price?

From the draft
  • The draft leaves this open: a separate implementation / onboarding fee, or the initial build absorbed into the monthly price and a term commitment (page 14).
  • The Quick Win, $1,500 one-time, already covers discovery, a baseline scorecard and a 90-day roadmap (page 3).
Options
  1. A separate one-time onboarding fee on Core and Pro.
  2. No fee. It is absorbed into the monthly price, paid from Strategy + Account Management, with a minimum term.
  3. No fee, but every partner does the Quick Win first, and that is the onboarding.

5

Health partners: ads and analytics

HeldDecided

Health and wellness partners have tighter rules on tracking and on what can be advertised. Are ads and analytics part of their package, an add-on, or left out?

From the draft
  • Core includes one primary ad channel and conversion tracking (page 5). The draft does not separate health partners from home services.
  • Its only health note: have counsel review any revenue-share or referral-linked pay for healthcare partners before it is used (page 14). That touches the sales commission.
Options
  1. Included at the same price, limited to what the rules allow.
  2. Available as an add-on for an additional fee, after a check of what that partner can advertise and track.
  3. Left out of health packages in v1.0.

Lee’s proposalOption b. The compliance setup is extra work that home-services partners don’t need, so it is priced on its own.
