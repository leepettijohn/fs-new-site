Price and billing
Price$895 a year, the current price.
RenewalRenews automatically every year through Stripe.
RefundsNone.
CancellingThe Member sends a cancellation request in the portal, so HHN can ask questions and offer more value before it's final.
$149 listingDropped.
Founding Members and referrals
Founding Members40% off à la carte marketing services (not the Core or Pro packages), and their price stays the same as long as they renew each year.
Referral payEvery Member can earn it, at one rate for everyone: $89.50 for now (the Sales Commission line).
Referral rules
  • Counts when the new business names the referrer on its application, or uses their link, and pays
  • Paid once per new Membership, first year only, within 30 days of the payment clearing
  • Not paid if that payment is refunded or reversed
  • No self-referrals, and no referring a second business you own
  • The referrer must be in good standing when it's paid; pay already earned is still paid after they leave
Joining
OrderHHN approves the application before the Member pays.
FaithThe statement of faith and the agreements are part of the application.
OnboardingOne step in the portal: send profile info, read benefits, confirm policies.
Profile fields
  • Business name: required, up to 60 characters
  • Category: required; 1 main category, up to 2 more
  • Short description: required, up to 160 characters (shows in the directory list)
  • Full description: required, 100 to 300 words
  • Services: required, up to 10, each up to 40 characters
  • Service area: required; cities or counties served
  • Street address: optional; if hidden, the map pin sits on the town center
  • Phone and email: required
  • Website: optional
  • Contact person: name and role; the Member chooses whether it shows
  • Logo: required; square, at least 400 x 400 px, PNG or JPG
  • Photos: optional; up to 6, landscape, at least 1200 px wide, 5 MB each at most
  • Social links: optional, up to 4
  • Prices: not shown. Reviews: not shown on HHN; a link to their Google reviews instead
Going liveThe Member approves a proof; the profile is live within 5 business days of HHN having everything.
UpdatesMembers send changes through the portal.
What a Member gets
Benefits
  • A profile page on healthyhome.network
  • A listing in the directory and on the map
  • Early-bird registration for Academy events (no discount)
  • The chance to host their own Academy event
  • Featured once a year in an educational panel or networking event: first come, first served, and the Member goes after it
  • The monthly Member newsletter
  • A monthly profile report: clicks and traffic
  • Referral pay
  • The HHN Member badge, usable anywhere, and the media kit
  • The Member portal: profile updates, benefits, billing and renewal
Said plainlyMembership is visibility and connection. Lead generation is a Growth Partner service, and Membership promises no number of leads, clicks or customers.
Events
Academy eventsPaid workshops open to the public that teach the trades (healthy-home inspecting for home inspectors, continuing education on mold for therapists). Members drive them: a Member who wants to teach one tells HHN.
Member perkMembers who aren't hosting get early-bird registration and payment, not a discount.
Free eventsEducational Panels and Coffee with a Pro are free and open to the public, for referral partners and business owners.

Who becomes a Member (draft)
Accepted when the business:
- Signs the statement of faith and the agreements
- Serves Middle Tennessee
- Offers a service that fits a healthy home (on HHN's category list)
- Holds every license and the insurance its work requires
- Has no unresolved pattern of complaints or poor reviews
Declined when any of these isn't met. Removed when one stops being true (see O4).

What “good standing” means, and the conduct rules

HeldDecided
Where it stands
  • Referral pay already depends on good standing.
Best guess to start from
Good standing and conduct (draft)
A Member is in good standing when:
- Their Membership is paid and current
- Their profile information is accurate
- They hold the licenses their work requires
- They have no unresolved complaint
When a complaint comes in:
1. HHN contacts the Member within 5 business days.
2. Outcome: resolved, a written warning, suspension (profile hidden), or removal.
3. The Member may appeal once, in writing, within 14 days.
