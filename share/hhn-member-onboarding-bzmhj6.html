The Overall Vision

Dusty, every new Member should know on day one exactly what HHN gives them and what HHN needs from them. Right now most of that lives in our conversations and planning notes. Once we settle what's below, I'll build it into the Lovable portal, so every new Member gets the same clear welcome and the same steps, and the reminders go out on their own instead of depending on one of us to remember. It also draws a clean line between Membership and Growth Partner work, so nobody joins expecting ads or leads from a directory listing.

Working assumptions

Nobody objected to these. Say so if any are wrong.

  • Members give accurate business details and tell HHN when they change.
  • Members respond when HHN needs something for their profile. The portal sends these requests automatically.
  • Basic support covers billing, profile fixes, portal access and questions about benefits.
  • Requests beyond Membership are quoted separately or pointed to Growth Partner services.
  • Academy events and referrals are opportunities, not requirements. No attendance or referral quotas.

Parked for later

  • Faith wording: older HHN text says it isn't a religious organization, which may not fit the 508(c)(1)(a) direction. Decide once that direction is final.
  • The final wording of the faith FAQ for Members.
