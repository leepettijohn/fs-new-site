  1. Found

    Switching your rentals page to Map View brings up the pins, but Google puts a message over the map reading "This page can't load Google Maps correctly", and the map itself is watermarked "For development purposes only" in grey across every tile. Google shows both of those when the billing account behind a map has lapsed or was never switched on. The map is also calling a retired version of Google's mapping service, which the browser console reports as RetiredVersion. On my own passes the rentals page went further and returned Results 0 with the property cards stuck as empty grey placeholders that never filled in.

    See it

    • https://www.centerhillchalets.com/center-hill-lake-rentals — then switch to Map View
    • https://www.centerhillchalets.com/center-hill-lake-rentals/hot-tub
    • https://www.centerhillchalets.com/search-location — eight marina areas offered as a text list, with no map

    Steps

    1. Open the Google Cloud project behind the map and check whether billing is active on it. The error and the watermark both clear once it is.
    2. Confirm the map's key is restricted to your own domain, so an active billing account can't be used by anyone else.
    3. Move the map off the retired version of the mapping service it currently loads.
    4. Open the rentals page in a browser you have never used on your own site and confirm the homes list fills in rather than sitting as empty placeholders.
    5. While you are in there, consider putting the same map on Search by Location — it currently offers eight marina areas as a text list, which is the one place on the site a guest is explicitly thinking about where a home sits.
