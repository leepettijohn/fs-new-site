After our meeting

What I heard, and what I need

Dan and Abby, thank you for Wednesday, and for the bread. Seeing Navigator, Planner and the seven-touch sheet side by side made the job clear.

This page has three parts: what I heard, the simple idea I’d like to build, and a short list of things I need from you. Tick an item and jot a note if that’s easier than an email, then send it over. Your notes stay on this phone or computer either way.

Please keep client names and policy details out of your notes, and out of anything you send me.

One

What I heard

Tell me if any of this is off.

  • The goal is seven touches a year with every client, spread through the year by last name.
  • The emails should sound like you, so they go out from Outlook in your own words, not from Navigator’s templates.
  • Navigator stays the source of truth. Nothing connects to it or pushes into it. Abby exports a client list, and that’s the only contact.
  • Reaching the client matters more than logging it. A simple list of what went out, and when, is enough.
  • This is a bridge until Salesforce arrives, so it should be small, quick to set up, and easy to walk away from.
Two

The simple idea

Nothing new to buy or install. It uses Excel, Word and Outlook, which you already have.

  1. Abby exports the client list from NavigatorOnce a month, into one Excel workbook.
  2. The workbook builds this month’s listWho is due, which of the seven touches they get, and who to skip because they already have that coverage.
  3. One push sends the emails from OutlookEach client gets their own email, by name, in your words. Replies come back to your inbox like any other.
  4. The workbook keeps the recordWho was sent what, and on which date.
