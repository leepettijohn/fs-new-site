There are no prices or percentages on this page. It only asks. Your answers come to me and nowhere else.

One

How it works

Every partner goes through the same five steps, in the same order.

  1. Intake and accessTheir signed agreement, a short questionnaire, access to their accounts, and their logos and photos. The partner keeps ownership of every account and grants me a role.
  2. BaselineWhere they stand today: Google Search Console, Google Analytics, their Google Business Profile, reviews, listings, and how they show up in AI search answers.
  3. ContentAn article on healthyhome.network and a different version on the partner’s own site, then social posts from it. Anything health-related gets a claims check first.
  4. AdsGoogle Ads, only once we can prove a lead is being counted correctly.
  5. Monthly reviewWhat happened, which leads became jobs, and no more than three changes for the next month.

Two kinds of partner

Home servicesMold, water, cleaning, building. The standard setup.
Health and wellnessTighter rules on tracking and on what Google will let us advertise. Some treatments can’t be advertised at all.

Where things will live

  • partners.healthyhome.network holds the pages partners open: the access walkthrough, forms, monthly reviews. Each partner gets direct links to their own pages.
  • The same site has a private area for pages between you and me, behind one code we both hold.
  • Articles meant to be found in search go on healthyhome.network and the partner’s own site.
  • Partners, tasks, content and links are tracked in one Airtable base.

How the shared content helps both sides

  • Trading links back and forth is something Google penalizes, so we won’t do that.
  • What works is real articles: a partner writes or is quoted as the expert, HHN publishes it, and each links to the other where it’s natural.
