3 things to fix on simplywatertn.com

What I looked at on October 1, 2026: Home, About, Products, FAQ, plus the request form behind “Get Your Free Water Analysis”.

  1. Your home page slides sideways on a small phone

    Found

    On a 320px-wide screen, your home page is 341px wide, so it scrolls sideways. I found the extra width in the “Do you know what’s in your water?” section, where the photo and the text block each run 21px past the right edge.

    See it

    • https://simplywatertn.com/

    Steps

    1. Open the home page on a narrow phone and scroll to “Do you know what’s in your water?”.
    2. In that section, set the photo and the text column so neither is wider than the screen.
    3. Reload the page at 320px wide and confirm it no longer moves left and right.

  2. Your site loads at two addresses instead of one

    Found

    I found that your whole site loads at both simplywatertn.com and www.simplywatertn.com. Neither address redirects to the other, and each one’s canonical tag names itself as the main address.

    See it

    • https://simplywatertn.com/
    • https://www.simplywatertn.com/

    Steps

    1. Decide which address is the main one. Your sitemap lists the one without “www”.
    2. Set a permanent redirect from the other address to the main one, for every page.
    3. Open both addresses again and confirm they end up at the same one.
