Start HereThe Overall VisionFYI

Cole, after Friday I’m even more convinced this is the right tool, and the right way to build it. Your company CRM is, in your words, “so close and yet so far”: logging one call takes a whole form, when your team makes hundreds a day. This is the opposite. One tap and it counts, on any phone or computer, with nothing to install and no license for anyone to buy. The points system your coaches gave you is built right in, so everyone sees their day add up and the whole team can see who’s putting in the work. Because it costs nothing per person, it can go wherever you take it: to a new recruit next week, or back to the coaches who gave you the idea, as a thank-you that actually helps them.

OneWhat’s being builtFYI

A web page your team opens on their phone or computer, laid out like the tiles in your CRM: each activity has a big live number with a plus and a minus. Tap plus and it counts. Tap minus if you tapped by mistake. Every tap is saved to a Google Sheet in your Workspace with the time, so later you can see when people are productive, not just how much.

What we settled on Friday

  • Built as a web page, not an AppSheet app. No license per person, so it can grow to other teams without growing a bill. Section Four explains how this changed the price.
  • Your points system, as you use it today. Points equal the letters in each code, and Dials are counted but not scored. The full list is in Section Two to confirm.
  • Sign up once with name, email and a PIN. The page remembers you after that. On a new phone, you just enter your email and PIN again.
  • Scores are public for accountability, with a leaderboard, and a switch in the Sheet to turn public scores off if you ever want to.
  • Any depth of team. Each person has a manager, and anyone above them sees their numbers, whether the team is 3 levels deep or 15.
  • Today, this week and this month for each person.
  • One Sheet for everyone, easy for you to filter and work with.
  • Ready for other teams later. Every person carries a team name, so another team can be added without rebuilding.
