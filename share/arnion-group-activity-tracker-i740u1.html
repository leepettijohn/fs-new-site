One

What’s being built

A phone app your team uses to log their day with one tap: a call made, a person talked to, an application submitted. Each tap is worth points. Everyone sees their own score for today, this week and this month. Mid admins see their own and their reps’. You see everyone’s.

Every tap is saved as its own line with the time, not just a running count. So later you can see when people are productive, not only how much.

What we settled on Tuesday

  • Built in Arnion Group’s Google Workspace, owned by you. I’m added as an editor so I can build and maintain it.
  • Three levels: you as top admin, mid admins, and reps. Admins log their own activity too.
  • Scores are private by role, unless you choose a leaderboard (Section Two).
  • Misclicks are easy to undo. Anyone can delete their own entries.
  • We start fresh. Your current sheet stays as an archive.
  • No public access. Only people you add can open it.

Six

Room to grow

Not part of this proposal, and nothing to decide now. These all build on the same app, using features that come with the AppSheet license your team will already have.

  • A daily recap. An email or phone notification to you and your mid admins with each person’s numbers at the end of the day.
  • Nudges. A reminder to anyone who hasn’t logged anything by a time you choose.
  • Charts and a dashboard. Activity by time of day, week over week, and team totals over time.
  • Goals. A daily or weekly target per person, with progress shown next to their score.
