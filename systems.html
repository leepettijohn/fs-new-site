When it's the right answer

You probably need a complex system if…

  • You're running your operations in a spreadsheet, and three different people have three different versions of it.
  • You're paying for software that does 20% of what you need, and you're working around the other 80%.
  • You've outgrown the free tier of three different apps and now you're paying $400/mo for tools that still don't talk to each other.
  • Your business has a workflow specific enough that off-the-shelf software will never quite fit.

When it isn't

You probably don't need one if…

  • You can describe your problem in one sentence and it sounds like "I need a website" or "I need a form that emails me." You want a Simple Build.
  • You haven't tried a simpler solution yet. Start with a Forward Proof.
  • You're shopping on price. The cheapest complex system is still $5,000, and it should be — there's no version of this that's worth doing badly.
